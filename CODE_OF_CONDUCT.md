# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, appearance, ability, or experience.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, empathetic, and accountable. Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **asnaripacalna@gmail.com**. Project maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy, and may correct, remove, or reject contributions or participation that violate these standards.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project.

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
